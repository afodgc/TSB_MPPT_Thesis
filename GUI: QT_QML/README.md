## How to learn QT
First, do you want QML or Qt widgets? QML is better for phones and QT widgets is better for PCs.  QML and Qt widgets are very different ways to describe the layout of your app. Learning one does not mean you know the other. Chose and then proceed the learning process.

The best way to learn the basics is watch the YouTube series from KDAB (link below).

It is approximately 18 hours (plus the hours of training by your self). Totally worth it. If you do not have time, See the first 6 videos and then skip to part 13 where you will actually start designing an app.

- YouTube tutorial series:
[Introduction to Qt Widgets](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6CJYn40gN6iFcTyItvnE5nOmJR8qk_7o&si=EP5tIRvgcgtBnPhN)

There are more options available. A lot of people online saying that the written tutorials from Qt academy are very good too (link below).

- Written tutorials: [Qt academy](https://www.qt.io/academy)
